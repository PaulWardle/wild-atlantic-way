What you can do
The home screen
Your hub: the countdown to kick-off, where the lads are right now, the map, and the latest messages and journal entries sliding through.
Where they are
Shows up only once the brothers post their location — and it refreshes every time they check in somewhere new. A quiet map just means they haven’t updated yet, so keep an eye out.

Slea Head, Dingle
The map
The shape of the whole journey — Malin Head down to Kinsale — with every headland and town, and the live pin marking where they are.
The postbox
Leave a hello, a tip, a recommendation or a question. Add your name, pick what it is, write your message — it lands on the home screen for the whole crew to see. This is your way to cheer them on.
RecommendationCommentQuestionFeedbackHello
The journal
A running, day-by-day record of the trip — locations, messages, milestones and notes with timestamps — that builds as they go. Watch it fill up on the home screen.
⚑ MilestoneRoadSignatureHere
Browse the route
Have a nose around: Days for the ride day by day, Today for what’s on now, and Sights & Roads for the headline spots and biker passes. Campsites and the day plans are all there to explore.
Tip: your messages and what you see are saved on this device. Keep the link handy to check back in.
Every screen & tool
Home
Your dashboard: countdown, live location, the map, the postbox and journal feeds, and the pack of tools.
We are here
Post your location so F&F can follow: pick the spot, add a comment, tap We are here. It flashes on the map and heads up the journal.
The ride (Days)
Every day’s stops with the detail. Mark each one Keep, Maybe or Cut as you settle the plan — the tallies roll up across the trip, feed Today, and drop into the Journal.
KeepMaybeCut
Today
The live day, set by the date — and it follows your Keep / Maybe / Cut calls: stops you’ve cut drop off the glance and kept ones get flagged as don’t-miss. Shows the call to make, tonight’s camp and a jump into the full day. Before the off it’s a live countdown.
Campsites
Every night’s pitch with backups and sell-out warnings. On the home grid and inside each day.
Sights & roads
Signature 15 — tick them off as you bag each one — and the 19 biker Passes, north to south. Bagged spots get a badge that shows on the guests’ version too.
★ Signature✓ Bagged
Kit & admin
The to-do list, full packing list, cost estimate, the bookings to lock in this week, and practical intel for the road.
The postbox
Everything F&F have posted. Read them and remove any you don’t want to keep. You review here — guests are the ones who post.
The journal
The full record. Add a note as Paul or CJ with a tag and a day, and it auto-pulls in your locations, Signature bags and the postbox — grouped day by day with times. Edit or remove your notes any time.
MilestoneOh Fuck! MomentKey EventPaulCJ
The map
The whole route with the live pin — from the home card or full-screen.
What guests see
Friends & family get a lighter, view-only version. They follow along — the countdown, the map and your live location, the postbox and the journal feed — and can leave you messages in the postbox. They can’t post locations, mark stops Keep/Maybe/Cut, see Kit & Admin or edit the record; Signature 15 & Passes show as a read-only “Sights & Roads” list.
Brother access
On the welcome screen, tap I’m a brother and enter the password WAW for the full planner. The Brother chip on the home header switches roles.
Reset & clear
Removes what you’ve added from the shared trip — for everyone. Grab a Backup from the Journal first if you want a copy.
