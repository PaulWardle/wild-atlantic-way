# Wild Atlantic Way — Route Rebuild

**Built directly on the official Fáilte Ireland route map** (KML, June 2023 — the actual signposted line, all 188 Discovery Points, all 15 Signature Discovery Points, in official route order).

Method: the official route geometry was stitched into a single south-bound line (2,507 km — matches the published ~2,500 km), every Discovery Point was snapped to it to get its **distance-along-route ("km mark")**, and the itinerary below was then built as nine *strictly forward* windows along that line. Every stop in every day is in true route order — no doubling back except deliberately labelled out-and-back spurs.

## 2. Hard constraints (unchanged)

- **Mon 10 Aug** — Steam Packet Douglas 09:30 → **Larne 14:15**. Riding starts ~14:30.
- **Mon 10 night — BOOKED: Binion Bay campsite, Binnion, Clonmany** (west Inishowen, official km ~120). The only fixed night.
- **Wed 19 Aug** — Stena **Rosslare → Fishguard 08:15** (booked). At port 07:15. So Tue 18 must end within ~30 min of Rosslare.
- August daylight: ridable ~06:15–21:00.
- Only night 1 is booked — every other site below should be phoned/booked **this week** (August = peak).

**The honest headline:** the full official line is 2,507 km (≈1,560 mi) + spur returns ≈ 1,700+ mi across 8½ riding days ≈ **190–200 mi/day at 28–32 mph coastal average**. That is a hard trip. It *works* in August daylight, but only with the release valves below pre-agreed — decide them at breakfast, not at 6pm mid-crisis.

**Independent verification:** this plan was adversarially checked by three separate review passes (route order / time arithmetic / coverage): verdicts **sound** on ordering and coverage, **fixable** on time — the fix being honesty about the finale: days 7, 8 and 9 are roughly **12 h, 12 h and 13½ h door-to-door back-to-back**. Individually each survives; together they are the price of the full Way in nine days. If that's too much, the pre-agreed valves (Mullet, Glencolmcille, the Carna loop, Valentia, Old Head spur) are the levers — pull them the night before, not mid-day. One bonus find: a **summer Buncrana–Rathmullan ferry** sometimes runs across Lough Swilly — if it's sailing in 2026 it saves ~25 mi on Day 2.

### Day 1 · Mon 10 Aug — Larne → Antrim Coast → Muff (WAW km 0) → Binion
**~150 mi · riding 14:30 → ~20:15 · camp BOOKED: Binion Bay campsite, Clonmany (km 120)**

Lead-in (Causeway Coastal Route, not WAW): Larne → A2 through Glenarm, Carnlough, Cushendun → **Torr Head (spur, +10 mi)** → Ballycastle → Bushmills → Coleraine → Derry.

Then the Wild Atlantic Way officially starts: **⚑ Muff (km 0)** → Moville → Greencastle → **Inishowen Head (km 9)** → Kinnagoe Bay (48) → Culdaff (61) → **★ Malin Head (101) — Banba's Crown, EIRE 80 sign, and Farren's Bar** (Ireland's most northerly pint, 5 min off the head) → Pollan Bay (116) → Ballyliffin → **Binion (120)**.

- Everything on Inishowen's east and north coast gets bagged **tonight, in forward order** — this is what fixes the "Farren's is backwards" problem. Tomorrow starts clean from the tent.
- **Release valves** (if the boat or A2 runs late — call it at Derry ~17:30): skip Torr Head (–45 min); or run Muff → R238 direct → Binion (–50 mi, –2 h) and do Malin Head as a morning spur from the tent tomorrow (+25 mi round, it's 12 mi from Binion). Nothing is lost, only re-timed.
